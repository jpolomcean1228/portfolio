Medicare compliance and ADR automation

Insight Ally — ADR Connect and R2 Case Management

A grounded compliance brain, a fleet of small agents that cross-check each other, and a system of record that does the deterministic math.

RegCheck Master is a 757-row RAG knowledge base for skilled nursing facility Medicare compliance, built in Smartsheet with a 200-question evaluation bank, a sniffer registry and gap analysis. On the document side I built and stress-tested ADR letter extraction — including an adversarial test document with deliberate traps across 20 fields and a companion answer key — plus automation specifications for PointClickCare in Playwright and NetHealth in Power Automate Desktop.

  • RAG pipeline
  • LLM-as-judge evaluation
  • Adversarial test generation
  • RPA specification
